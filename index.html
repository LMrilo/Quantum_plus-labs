Quantum+ Labs

Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore × i-LAMP Brescia

Interactive labs on quantum computing and quantum technologies: from programming qubits in Qiskit to quantum key distribution and single photons on a real optical bench.

10 labs 1 ECTS each Online: Labs 1–8 In person: Labs 9–10 Colab-ready notebooks

How to use

  1. Click Open lab on any card to open the lab's interactive page in your browser.
  2. Click Notebook (Colab) to run the lab's Python code in Google Colab. You need a Google account and an internet connection; nothing to install.
  3. Click Guide (PDF) for the lab's written guide.
  4. Labs 9–10 are pre-lab companions for a scheduled bench session at i-LAMP Brescia: go through the page and the notebook before your session.

Recommended browsers: Chrome, Firefox or Edge (recent versions). Layout and icons load from the internet; lab pages and PDFs open from this folder.
